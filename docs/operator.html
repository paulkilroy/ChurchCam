Camera & Stream · Operator Sheet

Going Live

Run the cameras and the YouTube stream, start to finish. Keep this at the desk.

1

Before the service

Turn on the power

Flip on the power strips. The ATEM, remote, and cameras all come on — check their lights.

Turn on the PC

In the web browser, YouTube Studio opens by itself to the Stream page. Make sure it's up.

Test the remote

Push the joystick — the preview camera should move. That's it, you're ready.

2

Run the cameras

On the ATEM

STEP 1

Pick the next shot

Press a camera on the ATEM. It loads onto Preview — the remote's screen switches to it.

STEP 2

Frame it

Use the joystick to pan, tilt, zoom, or tap R1 / R2 for a saved shot.

STEP 3

Put it live

When it looks right, press CUT on the ATEM to send Preview to Program (live).

STEP 4

Get ready again

While that camera is live, load the next one on Preview and frame it. Stay one shot ahead.

Go Live

  1. Make sure the YouTube Studio Stream page is up.
  2. Press ON AIR on the ATEM — it lights red. You're streaming.
  3. Glance at YouTube Studio to confirm it shows LIVE.

End the stream

  1. When the service is over, press OFF on the ATEM — streaming stops.
  2. Check YouTube Studio shows the stream has ended before you close anything.
Advancedonly when you need it — the silver Override button

Move the LIVE camera

Hold Override while using the joystick to adjust the camera that's on air right now. Move slowly — everyone watching sees it.

Save a preset

Point the camera exactly where you want it, then hold Override and tap R1 or R2 to store it. Tap that button any time to snap back.
