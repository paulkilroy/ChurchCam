ChurchCam · Olimex ESP32-PoE PTZ Controller

Operator & Setup Manual

ChurchCam is a joystick controller for pan/tilt/zoom cameras. It follows your ATEM switcher's preview bus — so you always drive whatever camera is cued next — and speaks both VISCA and ONVIF over Ethernet or Wi-Fi. This guide covers the on-screen display, the joystick and buttons, first-time setup, and the web configuration page.

1The screen at a glance

The main "console" screen shows everything you need while driving: which camera you're on and whether it's live, where the joystick is, the whole switcher bus, and whether the device is talking.

The console — a full breakdown of every region is in Reading the display.

2Joystick & buttons

Three physical controls do everything. The joystick drives the camera; the three buttons handle live override and presets.

ControlAction
Joystick — left / rightPan the camera
Joystick — up / downTilt the camera
Joystick — zoom axisZoom in / out
OVERRIDE button (hold)Drive the Program (live) camera instead of Preview — the tally box turns red. Also the "shift key" for saving presets.
PRESET 1 / 2 — tapRecall that preset (camera moves to the saved position)
OVERRIDE + PRESET 1/2Save the current position to that preset
Rule of thumb

A quick tap on a preset button recalls. To save, hold OVERRIDE and tap the preset button — you'll get a full-screen "PRESET SAVED" confirmation.

3Reading the display

Every colour and pill on the screen means something specific. Here's the whole vocabulary.

The header

  1. Signal — Wi-Fi strength bars, or a wired icon on Ethernet.
  2. Host & IP — the device's hostname, address, and Wi-Fi signal (dBm).
  3. ATEM — green pill when the switcher is connected; amber NO ATEM when it isn't.
  4. ON AIR — big and red when the switcher is livestreaming; dim OFF AIR otherwise — not the same as a camera being on Program.

The whole console

  1. Status header — signal, hostname/IP, ATEM link, and the big ON AIR / OFF AIR streaming indicator.
  2. Tally box — the camera you're driving; the frame is red on Program, green on Preview.
  3. Radar — live pan/tilt position (dot) and zoom (right rail).
  4. Camera bus — all 8 inputs at a glance, with tally and connection state.
  5. TX graph — commands the device is sending, per second.

Tally — Program vs Preview

The tally box frame tells you, at a glance, whether the camera you're driving is live:

FrameMeaning
PROGRAM (red)This camera is live on air. Move with care.
PREVIEW (green)This camera is cued next. Safe to reframe.
OFF (grey)Not on either bus.

The camera bus strip

All eight inputs, always visible. Each tile shows the input number, its tally, and whether a camera is actually reachable there:

TileState
red · PRGOn Program (live)
green · PVWOn Preview (next)
greyConnected, reachable
amber · !Configured but not responding
dimNo camera configured on this input

A white underline marks the tile you're currently driving.

Radar & TX graph

The radar crosshair shows live pan/tilt as a moving dot, with zoom on the right rail; both turn red when you're on a Program camera. The TX graph along the bottom is a running count of commands the device sends each second — a quiet heartbeat that lets you see it's actually talking to the cameras.

4Driving cameras & presets

You never pick a camera number by hand — the controller follows the switcher.

Which camera you're driving

By default the controller drives whatever camera is on the ATEM's Preview bus. Cue a camera to preview on the switcher, and the joystick moves that camera — the tally box shows it green (PREVIEW).

Need to nudge the shot that's live? Hold OVERRIDE — now you're driving the Program camera, and the tally box (and radar) turn red so you never forget the shot is on air. Release to go back to driving Preview.

Presets

  • Recall a preset — tap PRESET 1 or 2. The camera moves to the saved shot; the header flashes PRESET n RECALL.
  • Save a preset — frame the shot, then hold OVERRIDE and tap PRESET 1 or 2. A full-screen confirmation shows the camera and the position you saved.

Saving a preset — the confirmation

Holding OVERRIDE and tapping a preset saves the shot and takes over the whole screen for a couple of seconds. The joystick keeps working underneath, then the console returns.

  1. Which preset — the slot you just saved to.
  2. Camera — the number and name it was saved for.
  3. Position — the pan / tilt / zoom you stored, shown as bars.
  4. Recall — tap the same button later to return to this shot.

6First-time setup

Out of the box (or after an erase) the device has no network, so it hosts its own hotspot for configuration.

  1. Join its Wi-Fi — on your phone or laptop, connect to the ptz-setup network.
  2. Open the config page — browse to 192.168.4.1 (shown on screen).
  3. Enter your Wi-Fi, the ATEM's IP, and your cameras — then Save. The device reboots and joins your network.
After setup

Once it's on your network the screen shows the device's real IP and hostname (churchcam by default) — browse to that hostname or IP anytime to change settings.

Discover Cameras

Rather than typing addresses, hit Discover Cameras — the device probes the network (VISCA broadcast + ONVIF discovery) and lists what it finds so you can assign each to an input.

Logs

A live log with Info / Debug / Warn / Error filters — handy when a camera isn't responding and you want to see what the device is sending.

Firmware

Upload a new firmware file to update the device over the air. While it writes, the device screen shows a full-screen progress bar and a DO NOT POWER OFF warning, then reboots into the new version.

8Screen states & troubleshooting

When something's not normal, the screen changes entirely — it tells you what's wrong instead of showing a broken console.

Connecting / reconnecting

Waiting for the network. If it says Reconnecting, the link dropped and it's retrying — restart the device if you changed Wi-Fi settings.

No ATEM

The switcher is unreachable. You can still drive a camera directly, but there's no tally. Check the ATEM IP in Config.
