Restarting the controller...

Changed the Wi-Fi network?

If you just set a new Wi-Fi network, the controller has left the ptz-setup hotspot and joined it — so this page can't reach it here anymore. That's expected.

To get back in:

1. Reconnect this phone/laptop to your normal Wi-Fi.
2. Open http://ptz-setup.local — or type the IP address shown on the controller's screen into your browser.

If you only changed camera or ATEM settings, no need — it'll reconnect here on its own.
